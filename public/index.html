Why the name

Every request starts with prefill.

Before a language model writes a word, it reads your whole prompt in one forward pass and fills its KV cache. Then it decodes, one token at a time, until it's done. Those two phases behave very differently, and most of what makes an AI product feel quick, or cost less, comes down to handling both well.
